## 👋 关于我 / About Me

**中文**

你好，我是**空太**（KongTai），26 岁。一个扎根在 Minecraft 1.7.10 的原创模组作者、分支维护者、公益服务器服主，以及永远对神秘时代与植物魔法心怀敬意的玩家。

我的 1.7.10 情结始于 17 岁。那一年，神秘时代（Thaumcraft）与植物魔法（Botania）为我打开了一个完全不同的世界。我至今记得第一次在神秘时代里研究要素、第一次在植物魔法里用花田搭建产能系统的感觉——那不只是玩游戏，那是在一个由代码和想象力共同构筑的世界里生活。也正是从那时起，我决定无论版本如何更迭，我都会留在 1.7.10。

我没有计算机专业背景，学历是中专，英文也不算好。我能走到今天，靠的是对 1.7.10 的执念，以及 DeepSeek AI 在开发过程中给我的巨大帮助。我深知自己站在巨人的肩膀上，所以每一行我经手的分支代码、每一个我维护的模组，我都心怀感激。

**English**

Hi, I'm **KongTai** (空太), 26 years old. I'm an original mod creator, a branch maintainer, a public server owner, and a player who will always hold Thaumcraft and Botania in the highest regard — all of it rooted in Minecraft 1.7.10.

My 1.7.10 story began at 17. That was the year Thaumcraft and Botania showed me a world I didn't know games could build. Researching aspects, designing Botania mana systems — it never felt like just playing a game. It felt like living inside something made of code and imagination. Since then, I've decided that no matter how many versions Minecraft releases, I'll stay on 1.7.10.

I don't have a computer science background. I graduated from a vocational high school, and my English isn't perfect. What got me here is my attachment to 1.7.10, and the enormous help I've received from DeepSeek AI throughout development. I know I'm standing on the shoulders of giants, and I treat every branch I maintain with that awareness.

## ⚠️ 维护原则 · 请务必阅读 / Maintenance Philosophy · Please Read

**中文**

这是我最想让你在下载或使用我维护的任何分支之前，认真读一遍的部分。

**对于《神秘生存学》（我的原创作品）：**
有任何问题，请直接找我，我会负责到底。

**对于我维护的别人的作品（Fork）：**
请**不要**拿着我修复后的版本去找原作者反馈问题！原作者并不知道我改了哪些内容，他们无法、也不应该为我的改动负责。同样，也**不要**拿着原作者版本的问题来找我。
分支修复的本质是"在巨人的作品上做局部维护"，**不打扰原作者，也不越界替原作者承担责任**，是对所有原作开发者最基本的尊重。

**English**

This is the most important section. Please read it before downloading or using any branch I maintain.

**For "Thaumic Survival" (My original work):**
If you have any issues, please come directly to me. I will take full responsibility.

**For branches I maintain (Forks of others' work):**
Do **not** take my fixed branch and report issues to the original author. They don't know what I changed, and they cannot be held responsible for my modifications. Likewise, do **not** bring issues from the original author's version to me.
Branch fixing means "local maintenance on top of a giant's work." **Not disturbing the original author, and not overstepping**, is the most basic form of respect.
