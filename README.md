Hi, I'm **KongTai** (空太), 26 years old. I'm a mod branch maintainer, a public server owner, and a player who will always hold Thaumcraft and Botania in the highest regard — all of it rooted in Minecraft 1.7.10.

## 🧙 致敬巨人们 / Standing on the Shoulders of Giants

**中文**

如果没有 **Azanor**（神秘时代 Thaumcraft 的作者）和 **Vazkii**（植物魔法 Botania 的作者），我的 1.7.10 世界不会是今天的样子。

Azanor 用"六元论"（风、火、水、地、秩序、混沌）构建了一套真正有深度的魔法体系，让魔法不再只是武器和工具的包装，而是一套需要理解、研究和敬畏的知识[reference:0]。Vazkii 则用植物魔法证明了"自然"本身可以成为一套完整的技术语言，花、 mana、符文，每一个机制都经过精心设计[reference:1]。他们两位的作品，深刻地塑造了我对 Minecraft 模组的审美和世界观。

也感谢 **GTNH 团队** 开放的 RFG 构建模板。我不会向上层的高版本迁移，我会一直停留在 1.7.10，把这条路走到底。

**English**

My 1.7.10 world would not be what it is without **Azanor** (creator of Thaumcraft) and **Vazkii** (creator of Botania).

Azanor built a genuinely deep magic system around the "six aspects" — Aer, Ignis, Aqua, Terra, Ordo, and Perditio — turning magic into something that demanded understanding and respect, not just crafting recipes[reference:3]. Vazkii demonstrated with Botania that nature itself could become a complete technical language: flowers, mana, runes, every mechanic deliberately designed[reference:4]. Their work fundamentally shaped how I see Minecraft mods.

I'm also grateful to the **GTNH team** for making their RFG build template available. I will not migrate to higher versions. 1.7.10 is where I stay.

## ⚠️ 维护原则 · 请务必阅读 / Maintenance Philosophy · Please Read

**中文**

这是我最想让你在下载或使用我维护的任何分支之前，认真读一遍的部分。

**如果我维护的是别人的作品：**
请**不要**拿着我修复后的版本去找原作者反馈问题。原作者并不知道我改了哪些内容，他们无法、也不应该为我的改动负责。同样，也**不要**拿着原作者的版本问题来找我——那是原作者的作品，不是我的。

分支修复的本质是"在巨人的作品上做局部维护"，而不是"重新发明巨人的作品"。**不打扰原作者，也不越界替原作者承担责任**，是对 Azanor、Vazkii 以及所有原作开发者最基本的尊重。

**如果是我自己原创的作品：**
我会明确标注。这类作品的问题，欢迎直接找我。

**English**

This is the most important section. Please read it before downloading or using any branch I maintain.

**If I am maintaining someone else's mod:**
Do **not** take my fixed branch and report issues to the original author. The original author doesn't know what I changed, and they cannot and should not be held responsible for my modifications. Likewise, do **not** bring issues from the original author's version to me — that is their work, not mine.

A branch fix means "local maintenance on top of a giant's work," not "reinventing the giant's work." **Not disturbing the original author, and not overstepping to take responsibility for their code**, is the most basic form of respect for Azanor, Vazkii, and every original developer.

**If it is my own original work:**
I will label it clearly. Issues with that work are welcome to come directly to me.
